Drift is wear, not dust.

The stick module uses a carbon track and a wiper. The track wears, the resting position moves, and the console reads movement that isn't there. Cleaning can help for a week. Replacing the module and recalibrating fixes the cause.

  • Cursor or camera moves on its own
  • Character walks without input
  • Calibration drifts again after cleaning
  • Stick feels loose, gritty or clicks
  • Dead zone or one direction won't reach full range
  • Joy-Con, Switch Pro, DualSense, Xbox and more

Drift questions.

How much does Switch drift repair cost? Stick drift repair starts at $40 plus the replacement module. Diagnosis is free, and you get a written quote before any work starts.

How long does it take? Most controller repairs are 1–2 days once the part is in stock. We confirm the schedule before ordering.

Do you fix Joy-Con drift? Yes. Joy-Con, Switch Pro, DualSense, Xbox and other controllers get a new module, calibration, and a button and trigger test.

Can I mail a controller in? Yes. Controllers are small and cheap to ship. Send a request first and we'll confirm the address and return shipping cost.

Will cleaning fix it? Sometimes for a short while. Drift comes back when the module track is worn, so replacement is the durable fix.

Is it covered? Every repair includes a 90-day workmanship warranty. The replacement module also carries its supplier warranty.
