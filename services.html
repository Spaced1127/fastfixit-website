What we won't do.

  • Install or support pirated software, or mod a console for piracy.
  • Promise a fix before we've seen the device. A symptom is not a diagnosis.
  • Replace a whole board when a port or a trace can be repaired properly.
  • Hide the part price. It's on your written quote, with the part number.
  • Take on a job we don't have the parts, tools or experience to finish.

Not sure it's worth fixing?

Send the device and the symptom. Diagnosis is free, and we'll tell you what the repair involves and what it costs before you commit to anything.
