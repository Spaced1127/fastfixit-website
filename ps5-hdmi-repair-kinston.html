When the port is the problem.

An HDMI port takes the weight of the cable every time it is plugged in. The shell bends, the pins lift, and eventually the console loses the TV or only works when the cable is held at an angle. That is board-level work, not a board swap.

  • No picture, but the console powers on
  • TV says no signal or flashes on and off
  • Cable feels loose or falls out
  • Port pushed into the shell
  • Bent or broken pins
  • Picture works only at an angle

HDMI questions.

How much does PS5 HDMI repair cost in Kinston? HDMI port replacement starts at $100 plus the port. Diagnosis is free, and you get a written quote with the part price before any work starts.

Why is your HDMI repair cheaper than other shops? No storefront rent, no showroom, no counter staff. Storefront quotes are usually $200–$300; mine start at $100 plus the part. The microscope, rework station, test and 90-day warranty are the same.

How long does it take? Most HDMI jobs are 2–4 days after the replacement port arrives. We confirm the schedule before ordering anything.

Do I have to live in Kinston? No. Drop-off is by appointment in Kinston, and local pickup or drop-off can be arranged within about 20 miles. You can also mail the console in.

What if it's only the cable or the TV? We test the cable and display first. If the console is fine, diagnosis is still free and you won't be charged for a repair you don't need.

Is it covered? Every repair includes a 90-day workmanship warranty. The replacement port also carries its supplier warranty.

Do you fix Xbox too? Yes. Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PS4 and other consoles get the same port replacement and output test.
