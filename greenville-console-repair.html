Greenville questions.

Do I have to drive to Kinston? No. Greenville drop-off is by appointment on scheduled trips, and mail-in is available. Send a request and we'll confirm the next date or the shipping address.

How much does HDMI repair cost? HDMI port replacement starts at $100 plus the port. Diagnosis is free, and you get a written quote before any work starts.

How long does it take? Most HDMI jobs are 2–4 days after the part arrives. Greenville returns happen on the next trip, or we ship it back with tracking.

Can you meet on campus? We meet at a public location in the Greenville area by appointment. Bring the device in a bag with your contact details. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by ECU.

What if it's only the cable or the TV? We test the cable and display first. If the console is fine, diagnosis is still free and you won't be charged for a repair you don't need.

Is it covered? Every repair includes a 90-day workmanship warranty. The replacement part also carries its supplier warranty.
