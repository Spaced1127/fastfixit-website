Privacy.

What we collect when you send a repair request, why we need it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated

November 2026

What we collect

When you send the repair form, we receive the details you type: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your preferred contact method, the device type, model and serial number if you give them, the fault description, anything you tell us about previous work or liquid contact, and your optional answer to "How did you hear about us?"

We also record how you reached the form when that information is present: campaign parameters in the link (for example from an ad or a Facebook post) and the referring website's hostname. This is used only to understand which local advertising is working.

For a mail-in repair, we also receive the return shipping address, the carrier and tracking number if you add them, and a note of any accessories you are sending. If you add photos, we receive those too. We resize them in your browser before sending, so the uploaded copies are smaller than the originals on your phone.

Why we need it

We use your details to reply to the request, diagnose the device, prepare a quote, carry out the repair, and keep a record for the warranty and for our accounts. We don't need your account passwords, and we don't ask for them. If a test needs a login, you type it in on the device.

We don't use your details for marketing, and we don't sell or rent them to anyone.

Who sees it

The request is delivered through Cloudflare, which stores it in our private request inbox and provides the spam check, and through Resend, which sends the notification email. Those providers process the request on our behalf. Our email provider keeps a copy in the shop inbox.

The request inbox is not public and is not linked from the site. Access requires the shop's admin token, and access to the inbox and repair records is limited to the people who need it.

If we need a part or a specialist service, we may share the device details with that supplier. We won't share your contact details unless it's needed to complete the repair.

How long we keep it

Repair records are kept for the warranty period and for as long as tax and accounting rules require. A request that doesn't become a repair is deleted after 12 months. Photos are kept with the repair record unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't run advertising trackers. If Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, it reports aggregate page views without cookies or cross-site tracking. The spam check may set a cookie to tell a real visitor from a bot, and it is used only when you submit the form.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [EMAIL] or call [PHONE]. We may need to keep a repair record for warranty or tax reasons, and we'll tell you if that applies.

Security

The form is sent over HTTPS. Access to the shop inbox and repair records is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect; if you're worried about a specific document, tell us before you send it.

Changes

If this note changes, the date at the top changes with it. The current version is always on this page.
