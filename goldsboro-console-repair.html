Ask before you hand over a console.

A console repair is not a screen protector. The port is soldered to the board, and replacing it takes a microscope, hot air, and a rework station. These five questions will tell you what kind of shop you're dealing with.

Question Why it matters
Does the shop do board-level work in-house? If it gets shipped to a third party, you're paying a middleman and waiting longer.
Will I get a written quote with the part number? A price before work starts is the difference between a repair and an open tab.
Do you test the HDMI output after the repair? A port that looks fine can still fail under heat or a loaded signal.
What warranty do you give in writing? Workmanship should be covered, not just the part.
Can I see the bench? One bench, one tech, in-house. No mystery back room.
