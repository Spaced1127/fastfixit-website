Straight answers.

Do you charge to look at it? Yes. $45 for the bench diagnosis. It comes off the bill if you approve the repair. If we can't find the fault, you pay the bench fee only and get a written explanation.

How fast is fast? Diagnosis is usually 24–48 hours after drop-off. Most repairs are 2–5 business days after approval. Parts can add time; we tell you the day we know.

Do you use genuine parts? Genuine where they're available and make sense. Where they aren't, we use matched-spec parts and tell you which. The part number goes on your invoice.

Will I lose my data? Most hardware repairs don't touch storage. Back up anything you can't lose before you drop off. We never ask for your account passwords; if a test needs a login, you type it in.

Can you fix liquid damage? We assess it, clean the corrosion and repair what the current damaged. We won't promise a result before we see it. Liquid travels, and corrosion can hide under shields.

Do you work on modded consoles? Hardware repairs, yes. We don't install or support pirated software.

Do you fix HDMI ports? Yes. PS5 and Xbox HDMI ports are a common job. We replace them at board level under a microscope and test every output afterwards.

Do you ship? Yes, you can post a device in. Pack it well, include a note with your contact details, and we'll confirm the return shipping cost before we send it back.

What if it can't be fixed? You get a straight diagnosis and the device back. You pay the bench fee only, no repair charge. We'll tell you if a part is unavailable or the board is beyond economical repair.

What warranty do you give? 90 days on our workmanship. Parts carry the manufacturer's or supplier's warranty. Physical damage after the repair isn't covered.
