Four steps, no surprises.

You'll get a reference and the shipping address before you box anything. Nothing happens until you approve the quote.

1

Send the request.

Choose Mail it in, tell us what the device is doing, and add a photo if you have one. Add the return address so we know where it goes home.

2

We reply with the address and your reference.

Wait for that reply before you ship. It includes the bench address, packing notes and the reference to put inside the box.

3

Pack it and send it in.

Include the device, the charger and accessories you listed, and the reference. Reply with the carrier and tracking number so we can watch for it.

4

Diagnose, quote, repair, return.

Diagnosis is free. You get a written quote before work starts, then a 24-hour test and a 90-day warranty. We confirm return shipping before it goes back.

Pack it like it matters.

A little packing saves a lot of damage. We'll send the shipping address and the reference after your request.

What to include

  • The device and the fault details from your request
  • Charger, cable, controller or SD card if it belongs with it
  • A note inside the box with your reference
  • The original box, or a snug inner box inside a larger one
  • Padding on every side; nothing loose

Shipping and returns

You pay the inbound shipping. We quote the return shipping before we send it back, so there are no surprises on the way home.

  • Insure the shipment if the device is valuable; carrier damage isn't covered by us
  • Don't ship a swollen or damaged battery without talking to us first
  • Keep the tracking number and reply with it after you ship
  • We'll send tracking when the repaired device leaves the bench

Free diagnosis. Written quote first. 90-day warranty on every repair. If the device can't be fixed economically, you'll get a straight explanation and the device back.
